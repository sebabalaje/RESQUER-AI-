# Ethics, privacy, and limitations

RESQUER AI is an educational NLP prototype. It is **not** a clinical tool, suicide detector, diagnostic system, or emergency-response service.

- Analyze only text that is public and lawfully usable, or obtained with informed consent and permission.
- Do not bypass platform controls, scrape private accounts, infer consent from public visibility, or collect credentials.
- Minimize retention; do not store raw text or identifying metadata by default.
- A model score is not a diagnosis and must not be used as the sole basis for contacting family, employers, police, or other third parties.
- Any real-world referral workflow must be opt-in, transparent, human-reviewed, and designed with qualified mental-health professionals and relevant legal/ethics review.
- Before research claims, replace the synthetic corpus with a governed dataset, document provenance, evaluate precision/recall and calibration, and test for subgroup disparities and false negatives/positives.
- If a person is in immediate danger, use appropriate local emergency or crisis-support services rather than relying on this prototype.
